## projects

Some of my personal projects I'm currently working on

  • AeroTrips — A community directory of fly-in destinations in France: hundreds of airfields and the activities you can reach from them, for private pilots planning a trip.
  • Songline — A browser party game built on Spotify: a song plays, and players guess its release year to build a timeline in chronological order. Other phones in the room follow the game live over ultrasound, with no network or account needed.
  • Citation Needed — A single-player mobile card game where you manage a roster of famous historical figures and send them on jobs, where their real-life expertise, quirks and rivalries decide what happens.
  • Advent of Code — I try to take part in the challenge every year to stay sharp on coding and algorithms (and because it's fun), usually in Python.
